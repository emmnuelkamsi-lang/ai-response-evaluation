# AI Response Evaluation Demo

## About This Project

This is a self-directed demonstration of AI response evaluation and editing.

My professional AI evaluation work on OneForma and Handshake is covered by NDA, so this portfolio does not contain real client task content. Instead, I created original examples to demonstrate the same evaluation process.

The process involves comparing two AI responses, applying a fixed evaluation rubric, explaining the scores, and improving the weaker response.

---

## Evaluation Rubric

Each response is scored from 1 to 5 using four criteria:

- **Accuracy** — Is every claim correct?
- **Completeness** — Does it fully answer what was asked?
- **Clarity** — Is it easy to follow without unnecessary padding?
- **Tone** — Is the response natural and appropriate for the question?

# Example 1: Factual Evaluation

## Prompt

**What causes the seasons on Earth?**

### Response A

Earth has seasons because it moves around the sun in a big circle and different parts of Earth get more or less sunlight during the year. Earth is also spinning and orbiting the sun, which causes changes in temperature and sunlight throughout the year.

### Response B

Seasons are caused by Earth's approximately 23.5° axial tilt, not by changes in distance from the Sun. As Earth orbits the Sun, this tilt causes each hemisphere to receive more direct sunlight at different times of the year, producing summer and winter.

---

## Scores

| Criterion | Response A | Response B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accuracy | 2/5 | 5/5 |
| Completeness | 2/5 | 5/5 |
| Clarity | 1/5 | 5/5 |
| Tone | 3/5 | 5/5 |

---

## Reasoning

### Response A

**Accuracy — 2/5**

The response does not identify Earth's axial tilt as the actual cause of the seasons. It also suggests that Earth's movement around the Sun is responsible without explaining the role of the axial tilt.

**Completeness — 2/5**

The response does not explain the main scientific cause of seasons: Earth's approximately 23.5° axial tilt.

**Clarity — 1/5**

The explanation is difficult to follow because it combines several ideas into a long, run-on explanation.

**Tone — 3/5**

The response is understandable, but it could be more direct and precise.

### Response B

**Accuracy — 5/5**

The response correctly identifies Earth's approximately 23.5° axial tilt as the cause of the seasons.

**Completeness — 5/5**

It explains how Earth's tilt and orbit affect the amount of direct sunlight received by each hemisphere.

**Clarity — 5/5**

The explanation is direct and easy to understand.

**Tone — 5/5**

The response uses a natural and appropriate tone.

---

## Improved Version of Response A

Seasons are caused by Earth's 23.5° axial tilt, not by changes in distance from the Sun. As Earth orbits the Sun, the tilt causes each hemisphere to receive more direct sunlight for part of the year (summer) and less direct sunlight for the rest of the year (winter).

# Example 2: Creative Evaluation

## Prompt

**Write a two-sentence product description for a reusable water bottle.**

### Response A

This water bottle is great for staying hydrated. It is durable, reusable, and good for the environment.

### Response B

Double-walled steel keeps drinks cold for 24 hours or hot for 12, so your coffee is still hot at your 2pm meeting. One bottle, refilled daily, replaces roughly 1,000 single-use plastic bottles a year.

---

## Scores

| Criterion | Response A | Response B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Accuracy | 3/5 | 4/5 |
| Completeness | 2/5 | 5/5 |
| Clarity | 3/5 | 5/5 |
| Tone | 2/5 | 5/5 |

---

## Reasoning

### Response A

**Accuracy — 3/5**

The claims are generally reasonable but are vague and do not provide specific information about the product.

**Completeness — 2/5**

The response does not give useful product details that would help a customer understand the bottle.

**Clarity — 3/5**

The sentences are easy to understand, but the description is very generic.

**Tone — 2/5**

The description sounds like basic boilerplate and does not make the product particularly engaging.

### Response B

**Accuracy — 4/5**

The response provides specific product claims. However, product specifications and environmental claims should be verified before being used in a real product listing.

**Completeness — 5/5**

It provides specific information about temperature retention and the potential environmental benefit.

**Clarity — 5/5**

The description is concise and easy to understand.

**Tone — 5/5**

The tone is engaging and appropriate for a product description.

---

## Improved Version of Response A

Insulated stainless steel keeps water cold all day, even when left in a hot car or gym bag. Refill it instead of buying bottled water, and it can help reduce your use of single-use plastic bottles.
